# Stage: Understand

Read `runs/<day>/source.json` and write `runs/<day>/understanding.md`.

## Required Sections
- `## Problem` — What concrete issue or tension triggered the essay.
- `## Central point` — The author's main thesis in plain language.
- `## Evidence` — Separate what is shown/observed, what is opinion/claim, and any inference.
- `## Limits and open questions` — Caveats, blind spots, and what the essay does not prove.
- `## Angle` — The sharpest framing for an engineering audience, plus an analogy and where it breaks.
